The interactive map above generates the complete TractionLab kickoff checklist for your project: enter a kickoff date and every task and blocker across the 30-day sprint (Weeks 1–4) auto-dates through the Day-30 (first user) and Day-90 (first dollar) milestones, with the critical blockers flagged. Check tasks off as you go — progress saves per project on this device — and copy the whole thing into Basecamp.
