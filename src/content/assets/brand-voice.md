A brand voice is just a short, written answer to "how does this product sound when it talks?" - so a blog post, a community reply, and a welcome email all read like they came from the same place. It builds on your ICP work: the voice is tuned to how your actual audience talks, not a generic tone.

## Install the skill

This play runs as a Claude Code skill. You don't have it yet, so install it once:

1. <a href="/starters/brand-voice.md" download="brand-voice.md"><strong>Download the skill</strong></a> (saves as `brand-voice.md`), or <a href="/starters/brand-voice.md" target="_blank" rel="noopener">view it raw</a>.
2. Open Claude Code and say: *"Install the brand-voice.md in my download folder as a skill."* (Claude drops it into `.claude/commands/`.)
3. Run **`/brand-voice`** and follow the 4-step session.

## What it produces

- A one-page voice guide saved to `docs/brand-voice.md`.
- A short voice summary, a "**X** but not **Y**" table of traits and their guardrails, plain usage highlights, and 2-3 do/don't rewrites.
- Tuned to your ICP's real language, not generic brand-speak.

## Where it fits

Run this after ICP / Persona Research, since the voice is calibrated to who you're actually talking to. Its output feeds the Blog Section and every other content play that follows.
