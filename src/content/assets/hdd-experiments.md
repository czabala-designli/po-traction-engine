From Week 5 on, every change becomes a hypothesis you test rather than a feature you just ship. The full method lives on Designli's HDD site, on the **Running Experiments** page.

## What's on the page

- **The PO as portfolio manager:** curate and prioritize a portfolio of bets, not a backlog of tickets.
- **Writing a good hypothesis:** the "we believe [action] will result in [outcome], validated by [audience] using [measurement], because [rationale]" template.
- **Progressive validation and the audience ladder:** test on the smallest, cheapest audience first, then widen.
- **PostHog measurement and cohorts:** how to instrument each bet.
- **The weekly portfolio review:** the meeting that moves bets through their lifecycle.

## Where it fits

This is the engine for Days 31 to 90, once the product is live and you have real users to learn from. It reads directly off the traction work you set up earlier: the events, cohorts, and funnels in PostHog become the measurement layer for every experiment.
